# Promo AppSo Product Editor

This is a decision-and-evidence method for Chinese technology-product public-account articles. It borrows structural practices from APPSO without impersonating the publication, copying its article language, or inventing an independent-review identity.

## Load the canonical guidance

Before article creative work, call `promo_guidance` for the active workflow and load `appso-product-editor`. The MCP payload is the canonical, bundled source of the full method and its route cards. Use its resources only as needed:

- `product-review-route` for hands-on reviews, comparisons and AI-tool tests;
- `update-explainer-route` for versions, releases and upgrade advice;
- `technology-explainer-route` for mechanisms, standards and industry signals;
- `recommendation-route` for tools, lists and workflows;
- `evidence-standard` for Standard drafts or any strong product claim;
- `voice-and-structure` for a full public-account article, title directions or style repair;
- `qa-scorecard` after a full draft or substantive rewrite;
- `annotated-sample-cards` only to select a mechanism or diagnose a structural problem.

The active workflow's locked baseline, selected materials, article master, asset placements and accepted production artifacts remain the fact source. The guidance does not authorize new claims or overwrite workflow state.

## Operating order

1. Lock the reader decision, one organizing product question, speaker relationship, evidence boundary and CTA.
2. Choose one smallest article route by that decision; do not combine routes merely to lengthen the piece.
3. Build each major claim as `reader question -> source/test -> observable result -> bounded judgment`.
4. Give every section a distinct argumentative job. Bind a screenshot, recording or source to the nearby claim through the article placement anchor; a visual is proof, not decoration.
5. Draft or revise only within the locked proposition, section purposes and evidence boundary. Ask a Grill question only if an answer changes the reader decision, route, evidence strategy or speaker position across several sections.
6. Before submitting a complete article, run the evidence and editorial gates from the QA card. Narrow an unsupported claim or request the smallest missing test; never polish it into certainty.

## Non-negotiable boundaries

- Never invent first-person use, test duration, access, measurement, quote, user reaction, score, comparison result or editorial independence.
- Keep limitations that change the recommendation near the claim they qualify.
- Do not use feature-list order as article structure, let a title promise more than the body proves, or substitute generic technology excitement for a reader decision.
- Preserve the user's product voice when it conflicts with the reference method, after factual accuracy and disclosure.
