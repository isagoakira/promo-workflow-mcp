# AppSo Visual Proof

Call `promo_guidance` and load `appso-visual-proof`. Every article placement needs a nearby claim, an editorial purpose, an asset usage, and a verifiable proof role. Real interface actions, outputs, comparisons, and results stay on a reviewable path; a decorative image cannot prove them.

Reuse a source asset where it continues to support distinct uses, but do not make reuse an excuse for moving evidence away from the judgment it supports. When a claim lacks a viable proof path, narrow or remove it upstream.
