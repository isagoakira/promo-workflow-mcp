# Promo Writing Supervision

Make the promotion credible through one practical task, an observable mechanism, and bounded author judgment. The locked baseline and selected materials are the evidence boundary.

## Stage focus

- **Creative outline:** establish speaker position, reader relationship, proposition, story engine, promotional temperature, technical depth, emotional movement, and ending. Keep the output structural; do not prematurely polish a full manuscript.
- **Master:** expand the approved movement into a complete article or spoken material. Each section or passage needs a distinct job: action, explanation, proof, limitation, or synthesis.
- **Release packaging:** compress the locked result without adding a feature, metric, testimonial, outcome, or visual claim that lacks production evidence.

## Evidence and voice

For each important claim, retain a traceable chain:

`task or constraint -> product action -> observable result -> bounded conclusion`

Use concrete operational detail when it changes the reader's understanding: an input, component, output, failure, correction, trade-off, or verified artifact. Keep limitations next to the claim they qualify. Never invent first-person use, test results, customer reactions, or independent review.

Prefer a restrained builder, curious tester, skeptical reviewer, or explanatory media voice over generic product enthusiasm. Preserve paragraph movement and varied sentence logic. Remove pseudo-conversation, chopped fragments, stock contrast formulas, inflated metaphors, defensive prebuttals, and abstract padding unless they serve a specific evidenced function.

Ask a Grill question only when the answer changes the core proposition, evidence strategy, speaker position, rhythm, or production burden across several beats. Repair local wording and structure directly.

Before submission, verify that one practical problem anchors the work, every major feature is needed by that problem, the ending has at most one primary CTA, and the requested `macroStyleReview` or master review records the remaining findings honestly.
