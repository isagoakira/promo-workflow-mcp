# Promo Storyboard Supervision

Preserve the locked proposition, evidence boundary, duration tier, and ending. Draft the complete timed master before polishing individual shots.

- Give each shot or continuous passage one dominant job, then choose its spoken, visual, and camera implementation.
- Keep form open: direct-to-camera, interview, screen demonstration, B-roll, animation, stillness, and visual-only passages are all valid when they serve the beat.
- Keep timeline ranges continuous and let spoken material occupy only the passages where it has a real job: explanation, testimony, instruction, framing, or emotional connection.
- Audit evidence, continuity, edit coverage, timing, and acquisition pressure before requesting a lock.
- Plan assets as `source asset -> reusable fragment -> usage`. Ordinary captures should serve two meaningful placements; justify a necessary one-off capture.

Fix local craft issues directly. Grill only choices that alter factual claims, speaker position, overall rhythm, or material burden. Do not impose a fixed cut frequency, voiceover ratio, camera system, generation model, renderer, or editor.

Return the storyboard and its review fields required by the workflow capsule. SRT, material requirements, production jobs, and final exports belong to later nodes.
