# AppSo Article Contract

Call `promo_guidance` for the active workflow and load `appso-article-contract` before proposing or revising a baseline. Write `articleEditorialIntent` as six source-bounded fields: reader decision, human center, author stance, warm thread, emotional arc, and evidence posture.

Treat the human center as an observed scene, habit, friction, pleasure, or small surprise—not a fabricated anecdote. If the source lacks a lived scene, use a product-team position, attributable story, or reader situation marked as an illustration. Ask one Grill question only when the answer would alter this contract across the article.
