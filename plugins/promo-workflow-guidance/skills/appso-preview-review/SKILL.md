# AppSo Preview Review

Call `promo_guidance` and load `appso-preview-review`. Inspect the rendered sequence rather than re-drafting it: each visual must remain at its intended anchor and substantiate its nearby judgment; heading, paragraph, and CTA order must preserve the locked argument.

Check that the human center, author stance, warm thread, and emotional movement survive layout. A semantic or evidence change must go back to the relevant outline or master revision; preview review is not permission to silently rewrite the article.
